What's working

  • Video is the strongest format you have. 18 median organic reactions and a 2.90% engagement rate, ahead of every still creative type.
  • Instagram rewards the real thing. Photographs earn 15 median reactions there against 3 for designed graphics, at a 3.28% rate.
  • Instagram engagement runs well ahead of Facebook across every creative type, at 2.4% to 3.3%.
  • Facebook loves meeting the team. Provider introductions reach a median 1,933 people there, the widest of any still format.
  • Graphics earn the shares. On Facebook they average 3.33 shares per post, the most of anything measured.
  • Community news travels furthest. The Salem Health affiliation post reached 30,591 people with no spend behind it.

Where we grow next

  • More short video. Clips of 5 to 20 seconds are already the top performer, so raising the count is the clearest lift available.
  • Match creative to platform. Candid photography and video to Instagram, provider introductions and shareable graphics to Facebook.
  • Build a capture habit. A phone in hand on rounds, at new-hire welcomes and at community events turns everyday moments into the best posts.
  • Keep introducing the team. Provider spotlights travel furthest on Facebook and are an easy series to keep running.
  • Lean into Instagram's momentum. Views up 69% and followers up 54% make it the fastest-growing channel to feed.
