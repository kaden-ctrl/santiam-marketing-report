What's working

  • The staff are the content. A real photograph of people at work is the most reliable organic performer you have, at 15 median reactions.
  • Instagram is accelerating. Views up 69% and followers up 54%, at a 1.04% engagement rate, triple the main Facebook page.
  • Family Birth Center leads on engagement with a 3.26% rate, the strongest of any Meta account.
  • LinkedIn grows on zero spend. 4.4% engagement and 188 new followers with no budget behind it.
  • Community news travels. The Salem Health affiliation post reached 30,591 people unpaid.

Where we grow next

  • More of the camera. Lifting real photography toward half the calendar would raise organic engagement without additional spend.
  • Build a capture habit. A phone in hand on rounds, at new-hire welcomes and at every community event turns everyday moments into the best-performing posts.
  • Keep graphics doing what they do best. Dates, registration and awareness months earn the most shares per post at 2.31, and the share is the call to action.
  • Put spend behind the strongest format. Boosting real photography compounds a format that already wins organically.

Method. Facebook and Instagram data from Meta Business Suite → Insights, LinkedIn from Page Admin → Analytics. Pulled September 10, 2026. Creative comparison covers 188 posts published March 18 to August 31, Stories excluded; each post's creative was reviewed and sorted into real photograph, provider portrait card or designed graphic, and Meta's reported “from ads” portion of reach and reactions is subtracted so those figures are organic. Headline totals cover January 1 to August 31 and include paid. “Views” is Meta's definition, counting all content views including repeats.
