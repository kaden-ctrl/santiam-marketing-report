What the numbers say

Reach fell 65%, engagement nearly tripled

Facebook views dropped from 627K in January to 219K in August, while the engagement rate climbed from a 0.18% floor in April to 0.52% — the year's best month.

One post defined the year

The Salem Health affiliation announcement (Jan 19) reached 30,591 people with 197 reactions and 53 shares, entirely unpaid — more than double the next post.

The top posts by reactions are mostly bought

Juneteenth leads with 534 reactions, but 528 came from ads. Strip the boost and the organic leaderboard is all local substance: the affiliation, the Regence explainer, the Women's Center.

The small accounts outperform the flagship

Family Birth Center returns 3.26% engagement on 4% of the reach — ten times the main page. LinkedIn holds 4.4% on zero spend. X is dormant: 40 posts, 470 views, 3 likes.

What's working

  • Substantive local news. Affiliation updates, construction milestones and new providers are the only posts clearing 5,000 organic reach.
  • Rising engagement quality. Four of the last five Facebook months beat the spring floor.
  • Link clicks up 128% against the preceding 243 days — 49,322 in eight months.
  • LinkedIn's organic base. 4.4% engagement and steady growth with no budget behind it.

What we're working on

  • Arresting the Facebook reach slide. Steady across all eight months, not a one-month shock — it needs a content answer.
  • Rebalancing the boost budget. Spend sits on holiday greetings; move it behind service-line and access posts.
  • Growing Instagram's scale. Its 1.04% rate triples Facebook's, but it reaches 7% as many people.
  • Deciding on X, and confirming the August link-click drop is behaviour, not a tracking break.

Method. Facebook and Instagram from Meta Business Suite → Insights → Results, read as daily series and summed by calendar month; LinkedIn from Page Admin → Analytics; X from the public profile timeline. Pulled September 10, 2026. “Views” is Meta's definition — all content views including repeats, not unique reach; deduplicated reach was 1.1M (Facebook), 139K (Instagram), 46.2K (Family Birth Center). LinkedIn's own dashboard reports 4.4% because its formula also counts clicks.

X coverage is partial. X Analytics sits behind X Premium, which this account does not have, and the public timeline stops serving posts older than mid-May. The X row rests on a 40-post sample, May 25 – Sep 10, using public view counts.
